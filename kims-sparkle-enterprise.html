About the enterprise

Local hands, professional standards.

Kim's Sparkle Enterprise started with a simple frustration: too many cleaning jobs in and around Kirinyaga were rushed, inconsistent, or left half-done. We set out to fix that — building a small, well-trained team that shows up on time, brings the right equipment, and finishes every job to a standard we'd be happy to have in our own homes.

Today we serve households, landlords, offices, and event spaces with four core services: laundry, sofa cleaning, cabro and floor scrubbing, and carpet scrubbing. Whether it's a weekly laundry run or a full compound scrub before a big event, our approach stays the same — thorough work, fair pricing, and respect for your space.

What guides our work

  • Show up as promised. Agreed time, agreed scope, no surprises on arrival.
  • Right tool for the surface. Cabro, carpet, upholstery, and fabric each get their own method — never a one-size scrub.
  • Price before we start. You know the cost upfront — no inflated bills after the job is done.
  • Leave it better. A finished job should need nothing further from you but to enjoy the space.
